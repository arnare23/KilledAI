Indian AI Startup Jivi MedX Tops Global Medical LLM Leaderboard, Surpasses OpenAI and Google By eHealth Network - 04 June 2024

  • Share:

Indian healthcare AI startup Jivi has achieved a significant milestone by securing the number one position on the Open Medical LLM (Large Language Model) Leaderboard. Jivi MedX, the startup’s specialized medical LLM, has outperformed prominent models such as OpenAI’s GPT-4 and Google’s Med-PaLM 2.

Co-founded by former BharatPe Chief Product Officer, Ankur Jain and GV Sanjay Reddy, Chairman of Reddy Ventures, Jivi has quickly ascended to the top of the AI healthcare industry since its inception in January 2024. The leaderboard, hosted by the renowned AI platform Hugging Face, evaluates medical-specific LLMs based on their proficiency in answering medical questions from various exams and research contexts.

Jivi MedX achieved an impressive average score of 91.65 across nine benchmark categories. These categories include the US Medical Licensing Examination (USMLE), Indian medical entrance exams (AIIMS and NEET), and detailed assessments in clinical knowledge, medical genetics, and professional medicine. This high performance underscores the model’s capability in delivering accurate and reliable medical information.


“Our mission at Jivi is to harness artificial intelligence to enhance patient care. Jivi is revolutionizing primary healthcare through generative AI, making top-quality care accessible 24/7 at a fraction of the cost. Our platform accelerates diagnostics and ensures higher accuracy, enabling timely and precise treatment for all,” said Ankur Jain, Co-founder and CEO of Jivi.

Jivi’s success is largely attributed to its robust team of physicians, surgeons, AI engineers, and data scientists, all working collaboratively to advance healthcare technology. The company leverages a vast proprietary medical dataset, one of the largest globally, encompassing millions of medical research papers, journals, clinical notes, and other sources. Jivi MedX was developed using a specialized instruction fine-tuning algorithm called Odds Ratio Preference Optimization (ORPO), which enhances its ability to understand and process medical information accurately.

This groundbreaking achievement highlights Jivi’s potential to transform the healthcare industry by improving accessibility, affordability, and quality of care through cutting-edge AI technology. With Jivi MedX setting a new standard in medical LLMs, the startup is poised to make significant contributions to global healthcare innovation.
