Maxtronics Robotics

Maxtronics is a French company developing social humanoid robots to improve people’s lives. 

​

Combining cutting-edge engineering with a clear human-centered vision, we design humanoid robots with purpose.

 

Our mission is to build trustworthy, purposeful and impactful robots that empower professionals in research, education and healthcare.


Our vision of robotics

Humanoid robotics is entering a new era.

The advanced technological performance needs to go hand in hand with societal relevance and meaningful impact.

​

We make humanoid robots that are both reliable companions and trusted tools.

We believe that care is the future of technology. Our mission is deeply rooted in education and healthcare, where the human connection matters most. 

 

We work closely with our robots’ users to provide them humanoid robots which truly meet their needs. 

 

​For them, we build the next generation of humanoid robotics : trustworthy, purposeful and designed to serve people.​

For us, the future of robotics is not about replacing people. It is about empowering them.


Our story

Building on two decades of innovation in humanoid robotics, Maxtronics was established in France on August 28, 2025, to pursue the technological adventure of NAO, including product development, sales and customer support, and to shape its new generation NAO7. 

 

In July 2025, Maxvision Technology Corporation acquired the core assets of the Aldebaran, formerly SoftBank Robotics Europe, thehumanoid robotics pioneer founded in Paris in 2005.

 

Throughout the years, Aldebaran developed a unique know-how and created NAO, the first humanoid robots industrialized at scale with +20 000 units deployed worldwide, Pepper (+ 17 000), Plato (+3 000) andRomeo.

 

NAO has become a global reference platform contributing to more than 3600 scientific publications and countless academicprojects worldwide.

 

Today, Maxtronics carries this heritage forward with renewed ambition, opening a new chapter for the next/new generation of humanoid robotics.
