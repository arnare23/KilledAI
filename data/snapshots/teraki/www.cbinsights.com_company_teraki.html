About Teraki

Teraki develops mobility solutions using edge AI and machine learning within the automotive and robotics sectors. The company provides software development kits for integration, which improve existing sensing devices by allowing data preprocessing. Teraki's technology serves industries that need autonomous systems, such as automotive, industrial drones, and warehouse logistics. It was founded in 2014 and is based in Berlin, Germany.

Compare Teraki to Competitors

Deepen AI

Deepen AI provides data lifecycle tools and services for the machine learning and AI sectors, particularly in the realm of autonomous systems and robotics. Its offerings include annotation, calibration, and validation solutions for multi-sensor data, for use in autonomous vehicles and robotic systems. The company serves the autonomous vehicle industry, providing tools that integrate with existing data processing pipelines for data labeling and sensor calibration. It was founded in 2017 and is based in Santa Clara, California.

Sama

Sama specializes in ethical data annotation and model evaluation solutions for artificial intelligence, focusing on computer vision and generative AI. The company provides services including data annotation, model evaluation, and data curation. Sama's client base includes a number of Fortune 50 companies. It was founded in 2008 and is based in San Francisco, California.

Kognic

Kognic provides autonomy data annotation within the artificial intelligence and autonomous systems sectors. The company offers services that integrate human feedback into the autonomy data flywheel, focusing on functions such as trajectory ranking, multimodal grounding, and visual-language-action models. Kognic serves sectors that require sensor-fusion data for autonomous systems development and validation. Kognic was formerly known as Annotell. It was founded in 2018 and is based in Gothenburg, Sweden.

Haltian

Haltian is a global IoT company that specializes in smart building solutions and IoT technology platforms. The company offers the Empathic Building digital twin service to enhance smart buildings, alongside Thingsee, a platform that facilitates rapid IoT deployments on a mass scale. Additionally, Haltian provides New Product Development Services to assist companies in transforming ideas into market-ready products. It was founded in 2012 and is based in Oulu, Finland.

Wayve

Wayve develops artificial intelligence (AI) solutions for autonomous driving technology. Its main product, the Wayve AI Driver, supports various levels of driving automation without relying on high-definition maps. Its technology is designed to work across different vehicles and regions. It primarily serves the automotive industry, offering solutions to automakers for implementing various levels of driving automation. It was founded in 2017 and is based in London, United Kingdom.

Understand.ai

Understand.ai provides ground truth solutions for the automotive industry, focusing on autonomous vehicle development. The company has a ground truth platform that supports various levels of annotation complexity and volume, enabling project delivery. Understand.ai serves sectors involved in the development and validation of advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) and autonomous driving (AD) technologies. It was founded in 2017 and is based in Karlsruhe, Germany.
