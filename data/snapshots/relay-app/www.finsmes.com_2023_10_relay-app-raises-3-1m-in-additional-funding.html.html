Relay.app Raises $3.1M in Additional Funding

By FinSMEs

-

October 13, 2023
Facebook
Twitter
WhatsApp
Linkedin

    Relay.app, a San Francisco, CA-based company providing workflow automation products, raised $3.1M in additional funding.

    The round was led by Andreessen Horowitz.

    The company intends to use the funds to expand operations and its business reach.

    Led by Founder and CEO, Jacob Bank, Relay.app is an automation tool that combines AI assistance, human-in-the-loop collaboration, and a multiplayer experience to help teams save time on repeated tasks. It enables users to add AI extraction, summarization, or text generation to any step in a workflow. Relay.app also includes a human-in-the-loop ability to double check the output of automated steps.

    Customers include companies like Ramp, Skyflow, Warp, Motion, Lumos, and Tavus.
